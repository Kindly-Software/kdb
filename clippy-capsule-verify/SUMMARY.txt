QUICK START FOR DEVELOPERS
================================================================================

See the lint warning?
  "atomic `load` uses Relaxed ordering"

Quick Fix:
  load()    → Ordering::Acquire
  store()   → Ordering::Release
  swap()    → Ordering::AcqRel
  compare() → Ordering::SeqCst

Performance Gain: 5-20%

Exception: Metrics-only counters can use Relaxed with #[allow(...)]

Full Guide: See P2.1_ENHANCED_ERROR_MESSAGES.md
