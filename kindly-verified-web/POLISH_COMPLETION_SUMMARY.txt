ANIMATION SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

Page Load:
  fadeInOnLoad: 0.8s ease-out (full page opacity 0→1)

Hero Section:
  glowPulse: 2s ease-in-out infinite (crown icon)
    • drop-shadow glow intensity: 20px→40px→20px
    • scale effect: 1→1.05→1

Feature Cards:
  slideInUp: 0.6s ease-out
    • Card 1: 0.1s delay
    • Card 2: 0.2s delay
    • Card 3: 0.3s delay
    • Card 4: 0.4s delay
    • Card 5: 0.5s delay
    • Card 6: 0.6s delay
  Effect: translateY(30px→0), opacity(0→1)

Loading Screen:
  crownGlow: 2s ease-in-out infinite
    • drop-shadow: 20px→40px→20px
    • scale: 1→1.05→1

  spin: 1s linear infinite (spinner)
    • rotate: 0deg→360deg

  progress: 2s ease-in-out infinite (progress bar)
    • width: 0%→70%→100%

  fadeInText: 0.8s ease-out (staggered)
    • Text 1: 0s delay (main text)
    • Text 2: 0.2s delay (subtitle)
    • Text 3: 0.4s delay (accent)

Interactive:
  hover_lift: 0.3s cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)
    • translateY: 0→-4px on hover

All animations use:
  • GPU acceleration (transform, opacity only)
  • Cubic-bezier easing for professional feel
  • Staggering for visual hierarchy
  • Sub-second durations for responsiveness
